Great question. Here's a broad survey organized by category:

Time-Lord Systems (beyond profections/firdaria)

- Zodiacal Releasing — releasing from the Lot of Fortune/Spirit through bound lords (Schmidt/Hellenistic). Arguably the most requested Hellenistic technique
- Planetary Periods (Decennials) — another Hellenistic time-lord system
- Circumambulations / Directions — primary directions through the bounds

Returns & Revolutions

- Lunar Returns — chart for Moon returning to natal position (~monthly)
- Planetary Returns — Saturn return, Jupiter return, etc.
- Progressed Charts — secondary progressions (day-for-a-year), solar arc directions

Lots / Arabic Parts

- Lot of Fortune (you have Pars Fortunae already), but also Lot of Spirit, Lot of Eros, Lot of Necessity, Lot of Courage, etc.
— there are dozens in Hellenistic tradition
- Sect-aware formula switching (diurnal vs nocturnal)

Dignity & Condition

- Essential Dignity Table (full scoring: domicile +5, exaltation +4, triplicity +3, bound/term +2, face/decan +1)
- Triplicity rulers (day/night/participating — differs by tradition)
- Bounds/Terms (Egyptian, Ptolemaic)
- Accidental Dignity — angular/succedent/cadent, oriental/occidental, speed, retrograde, cazimi, combust, under the beams
- Sect analysis — in/out of sect, hayz, contrary to condition
- Phasis — heliacal rising/setting (visibility of planets relative to Sun)

Fixed Stars

- Fixed star conjunctions — Regulus, Algol, Spica, Fomalhaut, etc. conjunct natal planets/angles
- Parans with fixed stars — rising/setting with stars at same latitude

Eclipses

- Prenatal eclipse (Syzygy) — the lunation before birth
- Eclipse finder — solar/lunar eclipses in a date range, with natal contact analysis
- Saros cycles

Horary & Electional Helpers

- Planetary hours — which planet rules the current hour
- Planetary days — day ruler
- Void of Course Moon — Moon's last aspect before sign change
- Moon phase — exact phase, lunation cycle percentage, next new/full moon
- Cazimi/Combust/Under the Beams detection
- Besiegement — planet between malefics
- Collection of Light / Translation of Light

Rectification Helpers

- Primary Directions (Ptolemaic) — directed MC/Asc to promissors
- Prenatal Lunation (Trutine of Hermes)

Harmonic & Modern Techniques

- Harmonic charts — divide the zodiac by N (4th harmonic, 7th, 9th, etc.)
- Midpoint trees — all midpoints sorted by zodiac position, midpoint pictures
- Antiscia & Contra-antiscia — mirror points across Cancer/Capricorn axis
- Declination parallels & contra-parallels — "aspects in declination"

Mundane / Ingress

- Ingress charts — Aries ingress, solstice/equinox charts
- Jupiter-Saturn conjunctions (great mutations)
- Outer planet sign ingresses

Miscellaneous

- Temperament analysis — classical temperament from Asc, Moon, season, etc.
- Almuten figuris — the planet with the most essential dignity at key chart points
- Dorothean triplicity lords — for sect-based analysis
- Quickening of the soul — prenatal syzygy-based technique
- Eminence calculations — traditional scoring for chart "strength"

The Hellenistic ones (zodiacal releasing, lots, bounds, phasis, sect analysis, fixed stars) would probably resonate most with your project's direction given the existing profections/firdaria/dignity work.
