# Astrology descriptions: numbered house themes

## Meaning and scope

These twelve bilingual entries describe each numbered house’s thematic domain, applicable to any chart. They do not describe personality merely from having that house, require a planet placement, or blend planet, sign and house. Portuguese precedes equivalent English; each description has two lowercase-start sentences with direct address. Health, intimacy, birth, death and institutions are neutral topics, with no advice, diagnosis, personal prediction or promised outcome.
