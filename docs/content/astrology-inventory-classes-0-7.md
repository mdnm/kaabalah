## Editorial and provenance risks

- A6:615-646 is entirely private NOTAS. The earlier handoff's suggestion to reuse it is ineligible. The public house definitions A6:6-49 are a usable replacement for general house themes.
- A7 begins with combined Sun sign and Ascendant descriptions, not twelve standalone rising profiles. Cite A6 for standalone Ascendants.
- A3 mixes Sun and generic sign profiles. A4 is the clean source for signs themselves; A3's direct Sun headings support Sun placements.
- A5 repeats profiles while adding archetype mappings. These are separate teaching layers, not grounds to invent a placement's psychological level.
- Many classes include practices, biomedical claims, deterministic outcomes and gendered statements. These are inventoried for scope decisions, not authorized for descriptions. User constraints exclude practices, promises and invented outcomes.
- Images leave some tables incomplete in plain Markdown. No missing cell was inferred from image filenames.
