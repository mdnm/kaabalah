## Qualifications and root adjudications

- Conjunction: compatibility governs joint expression, and narrow orb only permits one energy to overshadow the other; no universally good/bad conjunction.
- Sextile: beneficial/constructive potential and facilitation, with tension if energy is not released. Class notes ranges are split to 928-930,938-939,942-943; certainty/outcome clauses sharing those lines are not adopted.
- Square: energizing/conflict/blockage depends on planets involved. Split class notes:200-206,209-210 excludes intervening clinical material; challenges and motivation are possibilities, not illness or unavoidable outcome.
- Trine: ease may mean less effort and, depending on theme, less appreciation; no promised fortune/wealth.
- Opposition: Mat resolved tension between poles, agreeing with class notes:405-406 and 1566. Class notes:287-290 supplies geometry but calls the relation harmony; harmony between positive/negative poles is audit context only, not substituted for tension or used for sign classification.
- Duodecile: actual SEMI-SEXTIL section supports adjustment/harmonization and the desire to restart/converge new possibilities at 625-631. No external-opportunity claim or guaranteed growth is substituted.
- Octile: action/resistance and dynamics within limits; source examples, predicted future crisis/avoidance and favorable results are unadopted.
- Trioctile: qualified natal effort to work through difficulties mentally and seek self-knowledge. Source emphasizes progressions more than natal; no future-discovery, clinical or therapeutic claims. Final EN idiom follows approved copy.
- Quincunx: adjustment gloss at 1563 plus philosophical mediation image 1434-1435, explicitly bridged tentatively to 150 degrees at 1452-1454. May represent/symbolically preserves inference, not a universal personal result. Health/cure clause beginning 1455 is excluded.

## Approved entry notes

### conjunction

- Adopted the shared/nearly shared position, joining of energies, compatibility qualification, and the possibility that one energy may overshadow the other at a narrow orb.
- Class notes lines 405-429 inspected but its metaphysical and temporal claims were not adopted.

### duodecile

- The API key is duodecile. Class notes' SEMI-SEXTIL section at 561-563 and body at 598-609 describe adjustment and harmonization at the angle used by this API key; the display copy uses semissextil/semisextile, following the section terminology, while the query key remains duodecile without new aliases.
- Class notes' later list conflicts in terminology: 1549 labels the 30-degree entry Decil or semi quintil and 1550 labels Semi sextil at 36 degrees. This naming discrepancy is recorded, not used to rename the API key or change calculations. Restarting and convergence of new possibilities are described as desire, not a promised growth outcome.
- Root reread class notes:625-631: the desire to restart and convergence of new possibilities are adopted directly; they are not transformed into an external opportunity or guaranteed growth outcome.

### octile

- The API key is octile. The SEMIQUADRATURA section describes action and resistance and says its dynamic qualities operate within defined limits; the profile keeps that general theme without the section's planetary examples.
- Class notes:784-791 discusses a future crisis, its possible avoidance, and favorable results. Those predictions and outcomes are excluded; the profile does not add a stress or aspect-pair condition.

### sextile

- Adopted Class notes' facilitator/beneficial potential and qualification that tension can arise if energy is not released. Class notes' constructive and facilitating description was used compatibly. At 942-943 only the facilitation clause is adopted; certainty and event/outcome clauses sharing those lines and continuing through 953 are excluded.
- Class notes' sign-pair examples and polarity classification at 165-190 were not adopted.

### square

- Adopted tension/conflict, challenge or solution, possible energizing/conflict/blockage effects, and motivation to overcome difficulties. Effects are qualified as possibilities.
- Class notes sign/modality lists at 211-213 and Class notes lines 1066-1152 were inspected but not used; the latter's fatalistic, health, and relationship claims are excluded.

### trine

- Adopted the harmony/benefit description and preserved the source's qualification that ease may mean less effort and, depending on the theme, less appreciation. No luck, wealth, or guaranteed outcome is claimed.
- Class notes' triplicity relationship is not included. Class notes lines 1292-1317 were inspected; its advice and fortune example were not adopted.

### trioctile

- The API key is trioctile; the source section is titled SESQUIQUADRATURA. Class notes:1377-1388 contains fractional-arithmetic errors and is not used for numeric prose. Class notes:1394-1396 gives the qualified natal clause about effort, difficulty, mental elaboration, and self-knowledge.
- Class notes:1392-1396 says this discussion is more prominent in progressions than natal. Health, disease, pain, therapy, future-event prediction, and specialist progression claims are excluded. The later phrase at 1405-1408 about "these three angles" has an ambiguous antecedent and is not adopted.

### quincunx

- The API key is quincunx. Class notes:1563 gives the short adjustment gloss; class notes:1434-1447 presents the mediation-between-opposites idea in a philosophical Jung/Plato discussion, so the copy marks this as symbolic rather than a guaranteed personal result.
- Class notes:1410-1414 and 1455-1466 attach health, disease, cure, therapy and illness outcomes to this aspect. All such claims, neighboring aspect types and any guaranteed result are excluded.
- Class notes:1452-1454 explicitly bridges the preceding philosophical image of opposites to the 150-degree aspect, tentatively. Its conditional symbolic status is preserved; the health clause beginning at1455 is excluded.

### opposition

- Class notes at 405-406 and 1566 defines opposition through tension between poles. Class notes at 287-290 instead describes a close relation and energetic harmony; following Mat's clarification, the harmony between positive/negative poles is recorded here in the audit only and is not inserted into the profile copy or used to classify signs.
