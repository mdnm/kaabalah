## Bridges and adjudications

The planet-house discussions are used selectively only where the source explicitly associates them with a MC sign. These are teaching analogies, not geometric angle/cusp identities or full equivalence. No generic planet/sign blend is generated. Root reviewed the actual adopted passages and approved the final claims. The primary-audit candidate list is advisory, not a prohibition on supported uncomfortable traits.

- Aries: body bridge at A9:1311-1312 supports selected public-image/ambition claims and explicitly retains the house of Mars as a qualifier of qualities shown publicly. No uncited initiative, leadership or autonomy claim.
- Taurus: standalone A9:904-906 retains determination after a career has begun, not a promised career outcome.
- Gemini: class A9:557-559 career-count statement concerns the house-10 cusp and is not adopted as MC. No career-count claim is transferred from the cusp.
- Virgo: heading A9:540 bridges Virgo MC to the selected work-precision phrase at 559; maternal/aspect continuation at 560-561 is not adopted. Book 66874-66878 independently supports method, detail, service and efficiency.
- Cancer and Leo: explicit closing body bridges A9:232 and A8:1421-1422 permit the selected public/work themes. Parental/childhood and local aspect-specific claims are omitted rather than generalized.
- Libra: standalone A9:909-915 retains cooperation, demands for matching precision/dedication, and sensitivity to possible future help. No guaranteed assistance or advancement. The service-contribution phrase at 910 is inspected but unadopted in prose and keywords.
- Scorpio: A10:2302 bridges the independent career passage 2283-2289. Some-case loss/abandonment/change and some-person institutional reform remain qualified. The maternal negative-projection branch at 2256-2267, called one-sided at 2268, is not adopted or detached from its antecedent.
- Sagittarius: A9:1760-1761 supplies the body bridge. At 1744-1745 the source says probably work better with authority/room; the corrected text retains may work better, not a preference for authority.
- Capricorn: A10:407-409 supplies an explicit body bridge and organization/administration. Judgment of people outside strict social values at 370-371 is faithfully retained. Well-aspected success at 374-375 and badly-aspected delays at 376 are not borrowed as unconditional MC traits.
- Aquarius: only standalone A10:896-898 is adopted: work benefiting many and room for inventive/idealistic expression. No job disruption, medical, parental or childhood narrative.
- Pisces: A10:1527 explicitly pairs Pisces MC with the Neptune-house-10 heading, but the assigned body has no separate Pisces-specific paragraph. This is a weaker, header-only bridge; root approves only selected career uncertainty and possible dissatisfaction with uninspiring work at 1545-1546.
