## Adopted qualifications and excluded claims

Only source-specific symbolic potentials are retained, not generic Chiron+sign/house blends. The source's clinical, fixed-childhood and therapist/healing material is excluded. No abuse/birth trauma/illness/parental history is assigned, no diagnosis or promised health/relationship outcome, no age/evolution assignment, practice or imperative. Excluding a source claim does not authorize inventing another cause, motive or feeling.

Aries retains independence/acceptance tension, gentler kindness/sociability and self-acceptance learning. Taurus distinguishes pain at loss from guilt about gain. Gemini attaches insecurity to ideas and keeps freedom of expression distinct, qualifying communicative strengths with difficulty expressing emotion. Cancer retains humanity/failure insecurity and community/wider-home roots without parent or displacement biography. Leo retains recognition versus creative pleasure and possible support for others' creativity. Virgo keeps exploitation as concern, not a predicted event.

Libra keeps cooperation/sharing as learning rather than innate preference. Scorpio retains tensions involving sexuality and spiritual values without saying they conflict with one another, and does not transfer another person's confidence into native trust. Sagittarius preserves differing-philosophy challenges and learning without assuming openness or fixed intolerance. Capricorn retains the value of different professional roles without inventing distinctive-contribution motives or promising rank. Aquarius names sensitivity around rejection without asserting rejection occurred. Pisces keeps collective perception and dreams' possible protective symbolism alongside practical life; adjacent prenatal/clinical clauses are expressly unadopted.
