## Review notes and source disagreements to retain

- a9:1574 labels the Jupiter house 6 chapter as Venus. Its body begins Jupiter at 1576 and belongs to the Jupiter sequence.
- a10:1839 labels Pluto house 4 as Scorpio MC; the body explicitly says Scorpio IC at 1888-1889. Treat heading as an internal error, do not invent a different interpretation.
- a12:69 labels Virgo as `VIAGEM`; the zodiac sequence establishes the intended slot, but preserve OCR review note.
- a12:1239-1264 node exaltation/fall placements disagree with a15:45-53. The inline editorial comment already flags this. The classes-over-book rule cannot resolve a class-versus-class conflict.
- a11:287-290 calls opposition energetically harmonious; a13:405-406 and 1566 call it tension. Record this if the opposition description draws from both classes.
- a13:1549-1550 swaps the labels/degrees of decile and semisextile relative to a13:561-570,635-643. a13:525 also calls a 20-degree angle vigintile while 556-557 gives 18 degrees. Do not copy numerical errors into API keys.
- a13:1377/1385 calls sesquisquare 2/8 even while 1382 correctly gives 135 degrees. a13:1418 has unreliable triseptile arithmetic. Geometrical prose is usable only after separating it from numerical OCR/error issues.
- a13:1256-1289 describes tridecile as creative development in this life, whereas a13:1559 labels it capacities from past lives. Requires synthesis or review, not silent replacement.
- a14:74-89 uses Cancer stomach / Leo heart; a14:156-163 uses Cancer heart / Leo stomach. Body-correspondence descriptions require Mat's decision.
- a15:269 calls quincunx 15 degrees despite the 150-degree discussion in a13. Treat as a likely missing zero and flag it rather than changing source.
- a15:280-283 gives a notably gentler Aries/house 1 Chiron account than a15:345-371. Both are before NOTAS; a synthesis must not discard the second or present adversity as a certain event.
- a15:28-46 explicitly inverts node labels by hemisphere. A consumer cannot silently apply these meanings to the library's astronomical node keys without reconciling that convention.
- Sun/Moon/other planet-house chapters often include deterministic health, death, sexuality, relationship or financial-event assertions and prescriptions. Their presence establishes rich source coverage but does not authorize those assertions in authored descriptions. Preserve the factor's supported symbolic/behavioral themes and track any entry that becomes too thin after exclusions as a gap.
- The handout itself insists at a13:1494-1498 that symbols must be understood and read together. Getters should offer factor text as context without claiming a full chart interpretation.

## Suggested contribution to the phase 1 proposal

Begin with 120 ordinary planet-house entries and the 96 Mercury-through-Pluto sign entries already present here, with the Sun/Moon sign sets supplied by the other classes. Then add 12 houses, 12 sign profiles, 4 elements, 3 modalities, 2 polarities, four angle concepts and sign-specific angles where confirmed across sources. Next add Fortune (1 general + 12 signs + 12 houses), Chiron (1 general and 12 combined source profiles, exposed through separately typed sign/house lookups only with disclosure), and 12 directed node axes after deciding the hemisphere convention. Major aspects can form a small early batch, but resolve the opposition discrepancy first. Keep minor aspects, timing, synastry and medical/karmic concept material as separate scope decisions. Symbolic degrees alone add 360 entries and deserve their own explicit scope choice.
